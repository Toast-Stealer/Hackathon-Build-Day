FOR SCHOOLS AND STUDENTS

Build Day is a guided creative day where students learn the basics of making a Roblox game, then get time to make their own idea.

THE SHORT VERSIONStudents learn
by making.

There is a plan for teachers and plenty of room for students to experiment, laugh, and surprise themselves.

A SIMPLE SCHOOL-DAY STRUCTURE

One day, broken
into four parts.

9:00

Welcome & setup

Students sign in, meet the facilitators, and learn the goals for the day.

10:00

Learn the basics

Short guided lessons introduce world-building, design, and safe collaboration.

12:00

Build & test

Students work on a small game idea, test it, and improve it with feedback.

2:00

Share & reflect

Teams present what they made and talk about the choices they made.

WHAT STUDENTS PRACTICE

More than
playing.

01Creative thinkingTurn an idea into a world with a beginning, middle, and goal.
02Problem-solvingTest, notice what does not work, and make a better version.
03CollaborationShare roles, listen to teammates, and give useful feedback.
04Digital confidenceLearn how tools can help students create, not only consume.

A PRACTICAL QUESTION

Who supervises
the day?

Build Day is designed to be run with the school’s normal adult supervision. Teachers or approved staff remain with students, help manage groups, and decide which devices and accounts may be used. The event provides the activity structure; the school keeps its usual safeguarding rules.

✓
